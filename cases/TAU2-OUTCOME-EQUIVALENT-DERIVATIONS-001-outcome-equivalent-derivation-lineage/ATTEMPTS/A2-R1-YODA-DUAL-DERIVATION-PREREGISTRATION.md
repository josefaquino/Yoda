## One-execution boundary

After:

```text
A2_EXECUTION_GATE=PASS
```

A2-R1 is consumed and must never be rerun.

Yoda persistence, verification, byte-recovery or relation-recovery failure after the gate is a scientific FAIL.

Non-Yoda infrastructure/materialization failure after the gate is NOT_EVALUATED, but the one-execution lock remains consumed and no rerun is allowed.
