# CASE — NVDA EVENT STUDY PLUS FORECAST ENGINE

Question 1: How much of the NVDA drawdown, June 24 to November 20 2026, is shared with the market and semiconductor sector?
Question 2: What price-risk distribution does a pre-specified, time-respecting 60-session simulation generate as of the November 20 close?
Question 3: Does historical walk-forward evidence justify considering a 125/105 February 19 2027 bear put debit spread at verified executable prices?

Core rules: no fabricated observations; no leakage of post-origin information; no claims of causal identification from simple event correlation; no prediction/trading claims from successful code execution; Yoda and Kyber are immutable dependencies, not targets of modification.

Primary observation study: 150 calendar days. Training data is additional earlier dates. Source prices have to be audited against corporate-action adjustments.
