## Interpretation

The product result is straightforward:

> KyberDB successfully transformed a real NCBI RefSeq genomic sequence into a durable k-mer frequency index and reconstructed exactly the same canonical state after verify, close and reopen.

The 100K stage recovered all 99,926 unique k-mers with zero missing keys and zero value mismatches.

The B1 performance measurements also show substantially higher verification and physical-storage cost than B0. That observation is retained as evidence; this case does not attribute the cause to a specific internal mechanism.

## What this case proves

For the tested stages, KyberDB provides a local embedded persistence layer for exact genomic subsequence identity and frequency with deterministic post-reopen reconstruction.

It does not prove biological analysis functions such as alignment, variant calling or genome assembly.
